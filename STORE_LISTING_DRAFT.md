# Nivium Store Listing Draft

## Positioning

Nivium is a field-ready snow profile app for quickly capturing, formatting, rendering, and sharing snowpack observations.

Core product themes:
- voice notes to structured snow profiles
- manual entry with a field-card workflow
- plotted snow profile output
- archive, review, and sharing on device

## App Store Copy

### App Name

Nivium

### Subtitle

Voice Notes to Snow Profiles

Alternate subtitle options:
- Snow Profiles in the Field
- Build and Share Snow Profiles
- Fast Snowpack Profile Capture

### Promotional Text

Capture snowpack observations in the field, turn voice notes into clean plotted profiles, and share polished outputs from your phone.

### Short Description

Capture voice notes or use manual entry to create clean snow profiles, save them in your archive, and share plotted outputs from the field.

### Full Description

Nivium helps snow professionals capture, format, and share snow profiles without fighting their tools in the field.

Use Voice Notes to turn spoken observations into a structured snow profile, or use the manual-entry workflow when you want tighter control. Review the result, save it to your archive, and generate a plotted profile you can share from your phone.

Nivium is built for practical field use:
- Voice Notes workflow for fast spoken profile capture
- Manual-entry workflow for deliberate field-card style entry
- Clean plotted profile output
- Archive for saved profiles
- Share workflow for sending finished profiles onward

Whether you are documenting a quick pit, building training examples, or capturing a profile to communicate with a team, Nivium keeps the workflow focused and readable.

- Manual-entry workflow for controlled field input
- Plotted profile output

## Screenshot Notes

For the strongest store images:
- use clean sample names and readable data
- avoid glitch examples or stress-test screenshots
- use your best-looking rendered profiles
- keep screenshots visually bright and legible
- prefer 4 to 6 strong screenshots over a larger weaker set

Best candidate sample names for screenshots:
- Test Profile 1
- Test Profile 2
- Test Profile 3
- one clean real-looking voice-note case

Avoid for store screenshots:
- debug overlays
- broken or crowded callout edge cases
- challenge-case names unless they look polished and natural
